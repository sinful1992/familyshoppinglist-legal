Why it beats a list on paper

It started with a scrunched-up list that went round the shop in a pocket, or got left on the kitchen table. The one thing nobody forgets is their phone.

One shared list

  • Everyone adds, everyone sees. Items show up on every phone in the family straight away.
  • Join with a code. One invitation code brings the household in.
  • No signal? Still works. It syncs when you're back online.
  • Urgent items notify the family, for when it's needed today.

What did we pay last time?

  • Photograph the receipt. Shop, date, total and each line are read for you.
  • Price history for each item, from your own shopping.
  • Compare shops for the things you buy most.
  • Every trip kept with its receipt.

Your shop, your budget

  • Your aisle order. Drag categories into the order you walk your shop, and the list follows it next time.
  • Monthly grocery budget with a warning as you get close.
  • Spending by category, by shop and over time.

Join the testing

The app is in closed testing on Google Play before it goes public. Testers get it free, and what you tell us shapes the release. Best of all: bring your household and use it for your real weekly shop for two weeks.

  1. Join the testers' group Use the Google account on your Android phone. Tester group link coming soon
  2. Accept the invite on Google Play Open the testing page with the same account and tap "Become a tester".
  3. Install, then create or join a family Make a family group and send the code to your household. They sign up, tap Join, enter the code, and you approve their request. Each of them needs steps 1 and 2 first.
  4. Use it for your weekly shop Please keep it installed for at least 14 days. Found a bug or have an idea? Post it in the testers' group.

Android only for now, sorry iPhone users.
